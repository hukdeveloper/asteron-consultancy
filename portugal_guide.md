Important Notes for Pakistani Applicants

DGES describes Portugal’s public higher-education network as including 14 public universities, alongside public polytechnic institutions and military/police higher-education institutions.

For a Master’s applicant, the exact programme page is more important than a general university page because deadlines, teaching language, IELTS/TOEFL requirements, MOI acceptance and documents can differ by faculty/programme.

If you want to apply without IELTS, do not assume that an English-medium Bachelor’s or MOI will be accepted. Obtain confirmation from the exact programme or follow its stated alternative language evidence.

Foreign academic documents may need Apostille/legalisation and certified translation according to the university’s rules.

2027/28 deadlines should be treated as pending until the official 2027/28 call is published. The 2026/27 dates in this document are reference dates only.
