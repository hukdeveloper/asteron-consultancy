## Total counts

- **Static pages:** 21 (17 real + 4 draft legal)
- **Dynamic detail pages:** 25 (6 + 7 + 3 + 6 + 1 + 2)
- **Total crawlable HTML routes:** 46, plus the custom `not-found` (404) page and error boundary
- **Sitemap entries:** static pages minus the 4 legal drafts, plus destinations/services/insurance/resources detail pages (25 − 1 − 2 = 22 dynamic entries included; events and scholarship detail pages excluded) — see `src/app/sitemap.test.ts` for the exact inclusion/exclusion assertions.
