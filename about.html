About Janan Consultancy

Janan Consultancy is a study-abroad guidance service that walks students through the full journey of applying to universities overseas — from shortlisting a course and checking eligibility, to tracking regional scholarships, preparing certified translations, and arranging visa insurance.

Our Italy guidance is fully live: a step-by-step application process, curated Bachelor's and Master's course listings, a directory of Italian public universities with real application links, and a region-by-region breakdown of regional scholarship bodies, deadlines and required documents.

We're actively building out guidance for Portugal, Germany, France, China and Russia. Until those are ready, reach out directly and we'll support you one on one.

Together, we rise — for a brighter future.
